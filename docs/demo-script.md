## ⏱️ 1:15 – 2:05 | Multi-Agent Execution & Live AI Telemetry

> **Narration (Voiceover):**  
> "Watch the execution pipeline in real-time:
>
> 1. **`BillingAgent`**: Calls `find_overdue_invoices()`, querying accounts-receivable ledgers and isolating overdue invoices.
> 2. **`CustomerAgent`**: Retrieves customer interaction memory and calculates dynamic collection priority scores based on payment reliability and aging debt.
> 3. **`CommunicationAgent`**: Invokes Google Gemini 3.6 Flash to synthesize empathetic yet firm reminder emails tailored to each debtor's aging tier.
>
> Notice the **Gemini 3.6 Flash Developer Inspector** dock at the bottom of the screen. We can open it at any moment to inspect the exact prompts, system instructions, latency in milliseconds, and structured JSON responses generated by Gemini."

**[Visual Cue: Expand bottom Gemini Developer Inspector drawer → show live prompt/response payload and 300ms latency pill]**

## ⏱️ 2:05 – 2:35 | Human-in-the-Loop Approval Gate

> **Narration (Voiceover):**  
> "Before a single email is dispatched, the system halts at an immutable safety gate.
>
> On the **Approvals** screen, the operations manager sees the customer details, invoice amount, aging days, and the exact email body generated by Gemini.
>
> The operator can inspect, modify, approve individually, or click **Approve All**. The moment approval is granted, the orchestrator immediately resumes execution."
